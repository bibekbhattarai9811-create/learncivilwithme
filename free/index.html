Cost breakdown

Is this roadmap free?

Yes. Every subject you need to learn has a free source, and no subscription is needed anywhere. The only unavoidable costs are the official exams.

Costs you can't avoid

  • FE Civil exam

    Paid to NCEES. Required for everyone.

    $225

    Year ~2

  • PE Civil exam + state license

    NCEES exam fee plus your state board's application fee.

    Several hundred $

    Year ~6+

  • FE-approved calculator

    TI-36X Pro or Casio fx-115ES PLUS.

    ~$20

    Now

FE fee checked on ncees.org, Sept 2026. Fees change, so check before you register.

Optional costs (you can skip all of these)

  • NCEES official FE practice exam

    Small fee. The closest thing to the real exam, so it's worth buying once near exam time.

  • OSHA 30, ACI, NICET certificates

    Help you get hired. Employers often pay for these, so ask first.

  • Autodesk certification exam

    Not needed. A portfolio proves the same skill for free.

  • Printed textbooks (Hibbeler, Das, McCormac…)

    Nice to have. Every subject here has a free textbook or video course instead.
