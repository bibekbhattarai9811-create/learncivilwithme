The complete path

Roadmap to Professional Civil Engineer

Nine phases, from high-school math to a PE license. Phases 3 (software) and 5 (industry) run in parallel with your studies. Getting paid civil work early is the fastest way through.

  1. 0
    PHASE 0 · Week 1

    Orientation & Setup

    0/6 tasks · ~5 h

    Understand the job, the licensing path, and set up a study system you'll actually stick to.

    What civil engineers actually doBuild your study system
  2. 1
    PHASE 1 · Months 1–4

    Math Foundations

    0/14 tasks · ~284 h

    Build the math every later subject depends on. Don't rush this phase.

    Algebra & TrigonometryCalculus I & IIMultivariable CalculusLinear AlgebraDifferential EquationsProbability & Statistics
  3. 2
    PHASE 2 · Months 4–8

    Engineering Fundamentals

    0/17 tasks · ~388 h

    Master the engineering science core. Statics and Mechanics of Materials are the most important courses you will take.

    Physics: MechanicsStatics ★DynamicsMechanics of Materials ★Fluid MechanicsEngineering MaterialsEngineering Economics & Ethics
  4. 3
    PHASE 3 · Months 2–12 (in parallel)

    Drawing & Software Skills

    0/12 tasks · ~144 h

    Get job-ready skills early. Software skills are how you get hired before you're licensed.

    Technical Drawing & Reading PlansAutoCADSpreadsheets for EngineersCivil 3DRevit / BIMStructural Analysis SoftwarePython & GIS
  5. 4
    PHASE 4 · Months 8–20

    Civil Engineering Core

    0/18 tasks · ~467 h

    Learn the actual civil design subjects covered in the FE Civil and PE Civil exams.

    Structural AnalysisReinforced Concrete Design (ACI 318)Steel Design (AISC 360)Geotechnical EngineeringHydraulics, Hydrology & EnvironmentalTransportation EngineeringSurveyingConstruction Engineering & Management
  6. 5
    PHASE 5 · Month 9 onward

    Get Into the Industry

    0/8 tasks · ~83 h

    Get paid work in civil engineering while you study. Experience is required for the license anyway.

    Entry certifications (paid, optional)Land your first civil job
  7. 6
    PHASE 6 · Months 10–24

    Portfolio Projects

    0/6 tasks · ~170 h

    Prove you can design. Each project = calcs + drawings + a short write-up on a web page.

    Six design projects
  8. 7
    PHASE 7 · Months 18–24

    FE Civil Exam

    0/6 tasks · ~127 h

    Pass the NCEES Fundamentals of Engineering (Civil) exam and become an Engineer Intern (EI/EIT).

    FE Civil preparation
  9. 8
    PHASE 8 · Years 2–10

    Credential & PE License

    0/7 tasks · ~4 h

    Complete a legal route to licensure and pass the PE Civil exam.

    Choose your licensure routePE Civil exam
