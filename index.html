How to study without a classroom

  1. 01Problems > videos. Spend two-thirds of every session solving problems with pencil and paper.
  2. 02Draw the free-body diagram. Every statics and structures problem starts with one. No exceptions.
  3. 03Units, always. Write units on every line. Most FE mistakes are unit mistakes.
  4. 04Rewrite your formula sheet. Once a week, from memory. Then check it.
  5. 05Get into the field early. A technician job teaches you what books can't — and counts toward licensure.
